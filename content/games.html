Dominoes

A very common cultural game for Caribbeans, Cubans included, is dominoes. From a very young age I learned to play dominoes from my mother, who learned to play from her father. It is a very simple game, but Cubans have a unique spin on dominoes. We play with a double nine set of dominoes instead of a double six set like other cultures. We almost never have a group of four in my family to play the team game with. Therefore, my mom and I usually just play a head to head version of Cuban dominoes. We typically play a mix of the traditional Cuban dominoes with a drawing aspect to make up for the lack of players. Some might say that our head to head version of Cuban dominoes is a bastardization. I would disagree, I think that because almost never have a group of four, we are making a cultural fun with what we have at our disposal. I think this limited cultural fun is exactly what Cubans do. The island of Cuba has had an embargo imposed by the U.S. government for over 60 years. Despite this, Cubans are still on the island. Is it a hard life to live, absolutely. The U.S. Government needs to stop the embargo, but I do not see it happening any time soon. I love playing dominoes with my family. I wish that I had a double nine set down here in Tucson, so I could play Cuban dominoes with my friends.
