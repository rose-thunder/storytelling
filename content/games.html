Dominoes

A very common cultural game for Caribbeans, Cubans included, is dominoes. From a very young age I learned to play dominoes from my mother, who learned to play from her father. It is a very simple game, but Cubans have a unique spin on dominoes. We play with a double nine set of dominoes instead of a double six set like other Caribbean cultures.

We almost never have a group of four in my family to play the team game with. Therefore, my mom and I usually just play a head to head version of Cuban dominoes. We typically play a mix of the traditional Cuban dominoes with a drawing aspect to make up for the lack of players. I wish I had a set of dominoes down here in Tucson so I could introduce my friends to this Cuban game.
